# Walmart Sales Dip Root Cause Analysis

This project investigates **why Walmart experiences sharp sales drops after holiday peaks** (e.g., Christmas → January).  
Using the Walmart dataset, I performed **root cause analysis**, created insights with Python & Power BI, and packaged findings in a boardroom-ready presentation.

---

## 1. Business Problem
Walmart sees **strong sales surges during holiday weeks** (Christmas, Thanksgiving, Super Bowl) but experiences **~50% sales dips immediately after**.  
This volatility erodes holiday gains and shows **over-reliance on seasonal demand**. Leadership requires clarity on *why* this happens and *how* to manage it better.

---

## 2. Objective
- Detect **holiday peaks** and **post-peak dips** across years.  
- Identify **key drivers**: promotions, seasonality, store types, departments, and macro factors.  
- Provide **boardroom-ready recommendations** to stabilize post-holiday revenue.

---

## 3. Requirements
- **Data**: Walmart dataset (train.csv, features.csv, stores.csv).  
- **Tools**: Python (Pandas, Matplotlib, Seaborn), Power BI, PowerPoint.  
- **Deliverables**: Jupyter notebook, interactive dashboard, executive slide deck.

---

## 4. Process
1. **Data Preparation** – merged sales, features, and store tables, added time features.  
2. **Trend Analysis** – plotted weekly sales to highlight holiday spikes & January dips.  
3. **Peak vs Dip Detection** – automated identification of Dec peaks and Jan lows.  
4. **Driver Comparison** – analyzed markdowns, CPI, unemployment, store types, departments.  
5. **Segmentation** – drilled down into store formats (A/B/C) and departments (toys, apparel, seasonal goods).  
6. **Root Cause Analysis** – created waterfall charts & root cause tree.  
7. **Boardroom Packaging** – built Power BI dashboard and executive slide deck.

---

## 5. Outcome
- Confirmed consistent **holiday peaks → January dips** across years.  
- Quantified **drop magnitude** and identified most affected departments and store types.  
- Delivered a **professional Power BI dashboard** and **5-slide deck** for leadership.

---

## 6. Results / Insights
- Sales dropped by **–41.3M in 2023** and **–37.2M in 2024** (peak → dip).  
- **Departments 1, 72, 5** (toys, seasonal goods, apparel) suffered the largest collapses.  
- **Store Types B & C** were hit harder than Type A.  
- **Promotions withdrawn (MarkDowns = 0)** → no buffer post-holiday.  
- **CPI ↑, unemployment ↓** → macro factors not primary drivers.

---

## 7. Business Value / Recommendations
1. **Smooth Promotions** – maintain baseline markdowns post-holiday.  
2. **Diversify Beyond Seasonal** – push grocery, household, and everyday essentials.  
3. **Target Store Types B & C** – deploy tailored post-holiday offers.  
4. **Value Messaging** – bundles, own-label, loyalty schemes to fight CPI squeeze.  
5. **Dashboard Monitoring** – set up Power BI alerts for abnormal post-peak dips.

---

## 8. Summary
This project confirms that Walmart’s **post-holiday dips are structural**:  
driven by **seasonality and promo withdrawal**, not macro weakness.  
By **smoothing markdowns, diversifying category focus, and tailoring by store format**, Walmart can stabilize revenue beyond seasonal surges.
